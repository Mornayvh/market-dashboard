# Weekly commentary

Written by hand each week, before the brief goes out. Two paragraphs: what
moved on the macro side, and what moved in the holdings. Leave a section
empty and its box is left off the page entirely — the brief still reads
correctly without it.

After editing, run:

    python update_commentary.py

That rewrites the commentary into the already-built pages in a second or so,
without refetching any market data, so the figures stay as at Friday's close.

Everything above this line is ignored. Only the two sections below are read.
